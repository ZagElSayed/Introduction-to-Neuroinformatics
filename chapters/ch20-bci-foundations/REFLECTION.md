# Explain the system, not only the score

1. Why do T1/T2 mean left/right here, and why would runs 06/10/14 change that interpretation?
2. Draw the distinction between neural phenomenon, recorded voltage, learned score, and authorized cursor command.
3. Why is whole-record forward/backward filtering unsuitable as evidence for a causal streaming implementation?
4. Which transformations were fitted, on which run, and what would constitute leakage?
5. What is the difference between a low model score, instructed rest, invalid signal, and an independently detected no-control state?
6. Explain the exposure denominator for the rest proxy. How could lost coverage make a rate look deceptively good?
7. Why can three overlapping score updates not be treated as three independent votes?
8. Which timestamp is recorded, which is simulated, and which duration is measured locally? What would a photodiode or actuator acknowledgment add?
9. What happens after each fault, and why are filter and policy evidence reset together?
10. What does replay establish? Design separate next steps for live feedback, cross-session generalization, and an accessibility evaluation.
