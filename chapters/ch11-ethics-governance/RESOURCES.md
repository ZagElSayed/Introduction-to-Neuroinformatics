# Chapter 11 — Primary Resources

Checked for this revision on 2026-10-14. Consult authoritative current materials and institutional experts for real projects.

| Resource | Read for | Apply to the lab |
|---|---|---|
| [Belmont Report](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/belmont-report/index.html) | Respect for persons, beneficence, justice | Distinguish the ethical question from a software permission |
| [HHS HIPAA and research](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/research/index.html) | Research use/disclosure pathways and authorization | Do not treat research consent, HIPAA authorization, and login access as interchangeable |
| [HHS de-identification guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/de-identification/index.html) | Safe Harbor and Expert Determination | Explain why assigning a study code is insufficient to establish de-identification |
| [OHRP research with children](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/regulations-and-policy/guidance/faq/children-research/index.html) | Permission, assent, and IRB responsibilities | Identify human/institutional determinations that the evaluator cannot make |
| [NIST SP 800-162](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/162/upd2/final) | Attribute-based access control | Trace subject, resource, operation, and contextual restrictions |
| [NIST SP 800-207](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-207) | Zero Trust architecture | Distinguish network location from sufficient authorization |
| [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.CSWP.29) | Govern, identify, protect, detect, respond, recover | Assign owners, incident actions, and residual-risk review |
| [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1) | AI lifecycle risk management | Complete model intended use, evaluation, oversight, and change control |
| [FDA clinical decision support guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software) | Function/intended-use-specific oversight | Avoid classifying software from a research-only label alone |

Chapter 9 establishes scientific meaning and provenance. Chapter 10 makes execution observable and recoverable. Chapter 11 adds authority for use, enforcement requirements, and accountable decisions. The synthetic evaluator exercises a bounded subset; it does not implement the complete trust architecture illustrated by the figures.
